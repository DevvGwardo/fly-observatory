    THE IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

    Real wiring.
    Honest boundaries.

    This is a connectome-driven controller, not a claim that a fly understands a game.

    The network is frozen. A trained linear decoder translates its activity into steering. The body, speed, and task encoding are engineered.

    What is real

    All 166,700 stored neurons and 25,582,938 signed connections run in a simplified leaky integrate-and-fire reservoir. Brain view shows a stable sample of 16,000 real recorded neuron coordinates. Bright points correspond to measured spikes.

    What is engineered

    The angle to the light and current turn velocity are encoded into cells wired to descending neurons. A ridge decoder is trained to reproduce a proportional-damping teacher. Seek and evade change the encoded task bearing. This is not retinal pixel vision or innate phototaxis.

    What this does not prove

    The brain-off control tests whether this controller uses its neural output. It does not show that biological wiring beats random wiring. That requires matched shuffled-network and no-brain controller benchmarks across seeds. We do not claim consciousness, learning inside the connectome, or biological fidelity.

    Timing and rendering

    The reservoir advances 20 ms per numerical step. Wall-clock playback may be slower. One clock serves every viewer. The detailed 3D body is procedural illustration, not a reconstruction. Coordinates preserve recorded relative geometry; the dataset is a whole nervous system, including the ventral cord.
